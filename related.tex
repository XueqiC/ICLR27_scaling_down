\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Compression scaling laws with a loss endpoint.}
Most compression laws predict an aggregate loss: sparsity as a
multiplicative factor on the size term \citep{frantar2023scaling},
precision through an effective-parameter count with a degradation term
that grows with pretraining tokens \citep{kumar2024scaling}, formats
used during training through one capacity scalar
\citep{panferov2025unified}, and a distilled student through a broken
power law in teacher loss \citep{busbridge2025distillation}. Closest to
our pruning arm, the P$^2$ law \citep{zhang2024scaling} predicts the
post-training loss of a pruned model from its size and loss before
pruning, the pruning rate, and the post-training tokens, validated on
held-out tokens, sizes, and rates; source-state conditioning is thus
not new in itself. None of these fit or validate a form on
per-capability measurements (\citet{busbridge2025distillation} report
downstream accuracy only as a proxy check).

\paragraph{Laws with downstream endpoints.}
\citet{sengupta2025compression} fit pruning laws on test loss and
averaged zero-shot accuracy against the compression ratio and recovery
tokens; the revised version of the same work adds rolling hold-out over
pruning ratios, zero- and one-shot transfer to unseen models, and
per-task exponents. Its inputs are the unpruned score and the pruning
ratio, with no source-state or training-history term, and its endpoint
is an accuracy or an inverse log-perplexity. Bit-width laws for
zero-shot accuracy exist at large scale \citep{dettmers2023case}, and
\citet{zhou2025task} stratify post-training quantization laws into
memorization, application, and reasoning strata for one quantizer,
validated on a held-out larger model and a second family; those laws
carry no source-state input and no recovery axis. Task-specific
distillation has been studied as curves over dataset size, compression
ratio, and supervision format without a fitted law
\citep{ghita2026task}. Relative to this line, the present study keeps
a per-capability conditional loss as the endpoint for three methods at
once, admits the dense capability vector and the training-token count
as inputs on a panel that separates size from training stage, and
reports frozen tests against baselines with the same information,
including the tests that fail.

\paragraph{Predictability of downstream capabilities.}
Downstream accuracy appears predictable mainly through continuous
log-likelihood measurements: emergent jumps are largely metric
artifacts \citep{schaeffer2023emergent}, accuracy-space prediction
degrades through information-destroying transforms
\citep{schaeffer2024predicting}, and successful pipelines fit
loss-space laws with sigmoid or exponential links
\citep{gadre2024language,bhagia2024establishing,owen2024how,ruan2024observational};
even then, linear loss-to-downstream relations hold in a minority of
settings \citep{lourie2025scaling}. The measurement layer of
\S\ref{sec:loss} follows these lessons.

\paragraph{Capability-selective effects of compression.}
Pruning disproportionately harms knowledge-intensive tasks
\citep{jaiswal2023compressing}, low-bit quantization disproportionately
harms reasoning \citep{liu2025quantization}, small-magnitude weights
encode hard-task knowledge whose removal is not recovered by further
training \citep{yin2023junk}, and selective low-rank truncation can
improve specific behaviors \citep{sharma2023laser}. These observations
motivate a capability-vector endpoint; the present paper adds
per-model, per-configuration measurements across families and a
prediction test of the resulting heterogeneity.

\paragraph{Gradients, curvature, and law forms.}
Second-order weight importance underlies classical and modern pruning
\citep{lecun1989optimal,hassibi1992second,frantar2023sparsegpt,sun2023simple},
Fisher-aligned subspaces improve compression choices \citep{fasc2026},
and gradient structure localizes skills
\citep{gurari2018gradient,zhao2024galore,michaud2023quantization,ilharco2023editing};
we use capability-conditional gradient and Fisher statistics only as
mechanism evidence (Appendix~\ref{sec:app_support}), never as inputs.
Surveys document the fragility of purely fitted scaling-law forms
\citep{li2025misfitting,besiroglu2024chinchilla}; the forms tested here
are low-dimensional candidates compared by held-out error and kept only
where they predict.
