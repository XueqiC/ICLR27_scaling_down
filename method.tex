\section{Study Design and Predictive Models}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Panels}
\label{sec:panels}

Three kinds of panel serve three purposes (Appendix
Table~\ref{tab:panels}). The \emph{heterogeneous panel} of 12 finished
public models from \nSeries{} series (Qwen3, Gemma-3, Gemma-4, Muse,
OLMo-3; 270M to 32B) establishes the phenomena and tests how far a
relation carries across families whose training history, data, and
architecture differ at once; family and provenance are confounded by
construction. The \emph{controlled Pythia panel}, sizes 160M, 410M,
and 1.4B at pretraining steps 16k, 64k, and 143k (nine states), varies
the source state along known axes with architecture and data fixed and
is the development set of every predictor; new sources for frozen
tests are 160M, 410M, and 1.4B at step 96k, 1B at 32k, 96k, and 112k,
and 6.9B at 32k and 112k. The \emph{distillation panels} use the nine
Pythia states as students with a fixed teacher pool, and Gemma-3-1B
with pools of 75, 225, and 600 traces per domain.

\subsection{Predictors that were tested}
\label{sec:predictors}

Every predictor was fit on the development set only, with
standardization constants, coefficients, and rules committed to a
register before any test measurement (Appendix~\ref{sec:app_register}).
Parameter counts are per capability.

\paragraph{Configuration-indicator source regression (source axis).}
At each intervention configuration $q$, a density or bit-width, a
separate linear model
$\widehat{\Delta L}_{c}(q)=\beta_{c,q}\cdot(1,\tilde{\log N_0},\tilde L_{0,c},\tilde{\log D_0})$
in standardized inputs, four coefficients per configuration. It
predicts a new source state at a \emph{seen} configuration and is not
a law over the intervention axis; its same-input comparator drops
$\tilde{\log D_0}$. It is the predictor frozen for the step-96k tests
(register \texttt{v38}) and, per bit-width, the quantization predictor
throughout.

\paragraph{Shared power form (pruning, configuration axis).}
$\widehat{\Delta L}_c(\mathbf x,d)=A_c(\mathbf x)\,((1-d)/0.3)^{\gamma_c}$
with $A_c(\mathbf x)=\beta_c\cdot(1,\tilde{\log N_0},\tilde L_{0,c},\tilde{\log D_0})$,
five parameters, fit on the nine development states at
$d\in\{0.9,0.8,0.7,0.6\}$ (registers \texttt{v40}, \texttt{v49}). Three
\emph{same-input alternatives} use the same inputs with a different
strength structure: A1, the power form with $\gamma_c=1$; A2, the
configuration-indicator regression at the four development densities
plus a fixed rule (linear interpolation between adjacent predicted
anchors, linear extension beyond the boundary pair); and a continuous
two-term polynomial in $1-d$. Three \emph{source-free baselines} ask
whether the source state is worth reading at all: a strength-only
curve $A((1-d)/0.3)^{\gamma}$ fit on all development rows, the median
development curve, and zero change.

\paragraph{Quantization baselines.} Per bit-width, the mean and median
of the development responses and zero change; the 5-bit prediction of
every candidate is a fixed rule, the average of its committed 4- and
6-bit predictions.

\paragraph{Distillation predictors.} On the source axis, a linear model
in $(\tilde{\log N_0},\tilde L_{0,c},\tilde{\log D_0})$, its no-$D_0$
variant, and per-capability constants. On the pool axis, four forms
fit on the 18 endpoint checkpoints of pools U75 and U600, a constant,
$a+b\log T$, $a+b\log E$, and $a+b\log T+c\log E$ with $E=T/D_U$ the
reuse count, all frozen and applied to the six U225 runs (register
\texttt{v41}).

\subsection{Validation protocol and labels}
\label{sec:protocol}

On the \emph{source axis} the intervention setting is seen and the
source state is not, through leave-one-stage-out folds or a frozen
prospective on a new source; on the \emph{configuration axis} the
setting is unseen (densities, bit-widths, pools), with or without a
new source. New-source tests follow a fixed order: forms and
standardization are committed; the dense capability vector of the new
source is measured, since it is a permitted input; every candidate's
predictions are written to a versioned file and committed with the
hash recorded; only then is the outcome measured. The last round uses
two fitting protocols, A on all nine development states and B on
states at step 64k or below, so that a 112k test stage is a stage
extrapolation under B.

Each comparison carries a four-part origin code (Table~\ref{tab:pred_main}):
whether the candidate prediction was frozen or is a leave-one-out
estimate, whether the same-input and the simple baseline were
pre-specified with it or computed afterwards, and whether all
pre-specified forms are reported. A frozen prediction keeps its label
whatever the outcome; where several baselines exist, the strongest per
capability is shown and marked as a retrospective choice. The only
post-test selection is the per-capability reading of the four
distillation forms, labeled as such. Which baseline answers which
question: zero change and the constant ask whether the response is
worth predicting; strength-only and per-configuration constants ask
whether the source state helps; the no-$D_0$ variant asks what $D_0$
adds; A1, A2, and the continuous form ask whether the power structure
adds anything. Errors are mean absolute errors in nats per token per
capability, improvement is baseline MAE minus candidate MAE (positive
means the candidate is better), bootstrap intervals are computed only
on the leave-one-out panels with source states as clusters, and
repeated probes, checkpoints of one run, and stages of one source are
never counted as independent sources.
