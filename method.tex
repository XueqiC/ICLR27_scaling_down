\section{Scaling-Down Laws}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Framework: scaling-down is selective deletion}
\label{sec:deletion}

Scaling \emph{up} adds capacity and data; the laws of
\citet{kaplan2020scaling,hoffmann2022training} describe what is gained.
Scaling \emph{down} deletes, and the three compression families delete
in three different bases:

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
Method & Deleted basis & Deletion order & Geometry \\
\midrule
Pruning & weight coordinates & magnitude, ascending & $\Fc{c,ii}\,w_i^2$ \\
Quantization & bit-planes & low-order bits first & $\operatorname{tr}\Fc{c}$ \\
Distillation & function components & capacity \& coverage cutoffs & teacher spectrum \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Compression as selective deletion. Recovery training is the
framework's only addition operator.}
\label{tab:bases}
\end{table}

What a capability loses under a method is determined by how that
capability's mass is distributed over the basis being deleted. Two
structural consequences follow immediately, both borne out by data:
no scalar compression ratio can be a universal damage coordinate
across methods (equal ratios in different bases delete different
capability mass), and distillation-recipe effects are \emph{coverage}
deletions of the training signal (removing chain-of-thought tokens
deletes the reasoning components; the prior grid shows exactly this
signature, \S\ref{sec:reanalysis}).

\subsection{Capability geometry: the mechanism object}
\label{sec:geometry}

All mechanism forms are functionals of two measurable objects at the
dense checkpoint, estimated from per-sample completion-loss gradients
$g_i$ over the estimation half of $\probeset{c}$:
\begin{equation}
\gbar{c}=\mathbb{E}_{i\sim c}[g_i], \qquad
\Fc{c}=\mathbb{E}_{i\sim c}[g_i g_i^{\top}].
\label{eq:geometry}
\end{equation}
Both are streamable at 30B scale: diagonal spectra are accumulated in
magnitude-quantile bins, and full quadratic forms with any $\dw$ follow
from streaming the scalars $g_i^{\top}\dw$
($\dw^{\top}\Fc{c}\dw=\mathbb{E}_i[(g_i^{\top}\dw)^2]$), so no large
object is ever stored. The first-order term is \emph{signed}: a
pretrained model is not at any single capability's optimum
($\gbar{c}\neq0$), so deletion can move a capability downhill---a
prediction unavailable to any positive-semidefinite
effective-parameter model \citep{frantar2023scaling,kumar2024scaling,
panferov2025unified} and, empirically, the term that separates families
(\S\ref{sec:mech}).

\subsection{The laws}
\label{sec:laws}

\paragraph{Dense reference.} Per capability, a Chinchilla-style
decomposition \citep{hoffmann2022training} anchors every arm:
\begin{equation}
\Lc{c}^{\text{dense}}(N)\;=\;E_c \;+\; A_c\,N^{-\alpha_c},
\label{eq:dense}
\end{equation}
with the exponent spectrum $\{\alpha_c\}$ itself a result: capabilities
that scale up differently should scale down differently.

\paragraph{Pruning (two regimes).} With deleted capability mass
$m_c(\dens)=\sum_{i\in\text{pruned}(\dens)}\Fc{c,ii}w_i^2$ and alignment
$a_c(\dens)=\gbar{c}^{\top}\dw_{\dens}$,
\begin{equation}
\Delta\Lc{c}(\dens)=
\begin{cases}
\varphi\big(a_c(\dens),\,m_c(\dens)\big), & \dens>\dens_c^{*}
\quad\text{(perturbative)}\\[2pt]
\text{collapse toward } \Lc{c}^{\varnothing}, & \dens\le\dens_c^{*}
\quad\text{(non-perturbative)},
\end{cases}
\label{eq:prune}
\end{equation}
where $\varphi$ is an empirical transfer function (second-order Taylor
is its leading form but is \emph{not} assumed exact
\citep{gong2025identifying}), and the collapse boundary
$\dens_c^{*}(N_0,f)$ is a fitted--and, we argue, ultimately
predictable--function. Empirically $\dens^{*}$ occupies a narrow
proportional band across a $115\times$ size range
(\S\ref{sec:curves}); its family dependence is the open frontier
(\S\ref{sec:family}).

\paragraph{Quantization.} Round-to-nearest at bit-width $\bits$ acts as
bit-plane noise:
\begin{equation}
\Delta\Lc{c}(\bits)\;=\;q_c\,4^{-\bits}, \qquad
q_c\propto\operatorname{tr}\Fc{c}\ \text{(mechanism form)},
\label{eq:quant}
\end{equation}
predicting exponential decay in bits with a capability-dependent
prefactor---and, jointly with \eqref{eq:prune}, explaining why pruning
and quantization share no common damage coordinate: one integrates the
spectrum's tail, the other its trace.

\paragraph{Distillation.} Per capability, a Busbridge-style axiomatic
template \citep{busbridge2025distillation} with two scaling-down
specifics: the teacher enters as a capability \emph{vector}
$\vec{L}^{\mathcal{T}}$ (prior data rejects any scalar teacher
sufficiency, \S\ref{sec:reanalysis}), and the recipe enters as a
coverage factor $\chi_c(\rho)\in[0,1]$---the fraction of capability-$c$
signal the recipe retains:
\begin{equation}
\Lc{c}^{S}(N_S,\Ds,\rho)\;=\;
\max\!\big(\Lc{c}^{\mathcal{T}},\,\Lc{c}^{\text{dense}}(N_S)\big)
\;+\;B_c\,\big(\chi_c(\rho)\,\Ds\big)^{-\beta_c}
\;+\;\Delta_c^{\text{gap}},
\label{eq:distill}
\end{equation}
where $\Delta_c^{\text{gap}}$ carries the capacity-gap correction. The
answer-only recipe's teacher-independent math deficit in prior data
($\chi_{\text{math}}\!\approx\!0$ when chains are stripped) is the
identifying observation.

\paragraph{Recovery.} The single addition operator obeys a
post-compression law \citep{zhang2024scaling,muralidharan2024compact}:
\begin{equation}
\Lc{c}(\Dr)\;=\;\Lc{c}^{\infty}\;+\;B'_c\,\Dr^{-\beta'_c},
\label{eq:recovery}
\end{equation}
with the mechanism-level prediction that $\beta'_c$ grows with the
overlap between recovery-data gradients and the deleted directions,
and a pre-registered \emph{state-function} hypothesis: the recovered
model depends on the damage geometry and $\Dr$, not the compression
path.

\paragraph{Link layer.} Task success is reported through
$A_c=g_c(\Lc{c})$, monotone and saturating, fit separately per
capability \citep{gadre2024language,bhagia2024establishing}; no law is
fit in accuracy space.

\subsection{Claim hierarchy}
\label{sec:claims}

\textbf{T1 (strong):} with deleted capability mass as the common
coordinate, one transfer function $\varphi$ covers pruning and
quantization, and distillation joins through the function basis.
\textbf{T2 (fallback):} the common coordinate holds but $\varphi$
splits by method---a unified framework producing a family of laws.
Either tier differs from single-method loss-space laws
\citep{frantar2023scaling,kumar2024scaling,busbridge2025distillation}
in five pre-registered respects: capability-vector $y$; a measured link
to task success; a recovery axis; coefficients constrained by
independently measured geometry; and cross-method comparability at
matched deleted mass. Falsifiers are declared with each mechanism
claim; \S\ref{sec:family} reports one pre-registered candidate
(top-concentration as a robustness predictor) that the data retired.

\subsection{Fitting and validation protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

Functional forms and keep/reject rules are committed (with timestamps)
before fits; model comparison uses repeated held-out
\emph{extrapolation} error---largest $N_0$, deepest compression,
largest $\Dr$---never in-sample fit indices
\citep{li2025misfitting,besiroglu2024chinchilla}; fits are weighted by
measurement error and uncertainty is reported from cluster bootstrap
at the configuration level; positive controls (synthetic laws must be
recovered) and negative controls (shuffled capability labels must
yield no structure) run alongside every fit.

\subsection{Deliverable: the method-selection map}
\label{sec:map}

Intersecting the per-method reduced-form laws at matched compression
cost partitions the (compression ratio $\times$ capability
requirement) plane into regions where pruning, quantization,
distillation, or a composition is the cheapest way to retain a required
capability profile---including the measured region where mild pruning
is better than free for retrieval-style capabilities on some families.
This map, in basic parameters only, is the practitioner-facing output
of scaling-down laws.
