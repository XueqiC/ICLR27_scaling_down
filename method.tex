\section{Framework: Selective Deletion and Capability Geometry}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Scaling-down as selective deletion}
\label{sec:deletion}

The three compression families act in three different bases, but all of them
\emph{delete}:
\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
Method & Deleted basis & Deletion order & Geometry \\
\midrule
Pruning & weight coordinates & magnitude, ascending & $\Fc{c,ii}\,w_i^2$ \\
Quantization & bit-planes & low-order bits first & $\operatorname{tr}\Fc{c}$ \\
Distillation & function components & capacity \& coverage cutoffs & teacher spectrum \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Compression as selective deletion in three bases. Recovery training
is the framework's only addition operator.}
\label{tab:bases}
\end{table}

A capability's fate under a method is determined by how its mass distributes
over the basis being deleted. This immediately yields a structural
prediction, confirmed in prior data: \emph{no scalar compression ratio can
be a universal damage coordinate across methods}, because equal ratios in
different bases delete different amounts of capability mass. The correct
cross-method coordinate is the deleted mass itself.

For distillation, the deletion view interprets recipe effects as
\emph{coverage} deletions of the training signal: removing chain-of-thought
tokens from transfer traces (answer-only distillation) deletes the reasoning
components of the signal, and indeed produces a large, teacher-independent
math deficit in prior data ($\approx-2.55$ ability units) while other
recipes at the same capacity are benign. The teacher enters through its
capability \emph{vector} $\vec{L}^{\mathcal{T}}$ rather than any scalar
ability---a hypothesis our re-analysis supports: stratified by recipe, the
same-size self-distillation anchor holds for standard recipes, and the
teacher--student capacity gap carries predictive weight only in isolated
capability--recipe cells (\S\ref{sec:exp}).

\subsection{Capability geometry}
\label{sec:geometry}

The framework's central object is measured at the dense checkpoint $\wz$
with forward--backward passes only. For each capability $c$, with
per-sample completion-loss gradients $g_i=\nabla_w \ell_i(\wz)$ over the
estimation half of $\probeset{c}$:
\begin{equation}
\gbar{c} = \mathbb{E}_{i\sim c}[\,g_i\,], \qquad
\Fc{c} = \mathbb{E}_{i\sim c}[\,g_i g_i^{\top}\,].
\label{eq:geometry}
\end{equation}
Both are streamable. The diagonal of $\Fc{c}$ together with $\wz$ gives the
\emph{capability spectrum} $s_c = \operatorname{sort}(\Fc{c,ii} w_i^2)$ in
magnitude-pruning order; quadratic forms with arbitrary $\dw$, including all
off-diagonal structure, are computable exactly as
$\dw^{\top}\Fc{c}\,\dw = \mathbb{E}_i[(g_i^{\top}\dw)^2]$ by streaming the
scalars $g_i^{\top}\dw$---no large object is ever stored.

\paragraph{Damage prediction (perturbative regime).}
For same-architecture compression, the second-order model is
\begin{equation}
\Delta \Lc{c}(\dw) \;\approx\; \underbrace{\gbar{c}^{\top}\dw}_{\text{signed
alignment}} \;+\; \tfrac{1}{2}\,\dw^{\top}\Fc{c}\,\dw .
\label{eq:taylor}
\end{equation}
Two features distinguish \eqref{eq:taylor} from effective-parameter
multipliers \citep{frantar2023scaling,kumar2024precision,panferov2025unified}.
First, it is \emph{per-capability}. Second, it has a \emph{signed} first-order
term: a pretrained model is not at any single capability's optimum, so
$\gbar{c}\ne 0$, and deletion can move a capability \emph{downhill}. This
term is invisible to any positive-semidefinite multiplier model, yet it is
what predicts the mild-pruning \emph{improvement} of multi-hop QA that we
observe (\S\ref{sec:exp}), a capability-selective analogue of low-rank
truncation gains \citep{sharma2023laser}. For magnitude pruning the
mechanism form specializes to the zero-free-parameter partial sum
$D_c(\dens)=\sum_{i\in\text{pruned}(\dens)} \Fc{c,ii} w_i^2$; for
round-to-nearest quantization at bit-width $\bits$ it gives
$D_c(\bits)\propto 4^{-\bits}\operatorname{tr}\Fc{c}$. Because second-order
expansions are known to underestimate quantization effects at realistic
magnitudes \citep{gong2025identifying}, the framework treats
\eqref{eq:taylor} as a predictive feature entering an empirical transfer
function $\varphi$, whose sharing across methods and capabilities is itself
a pre-registered hypothesis (below).

\subsection{Two regimes: perturbation and collapse}
\label{sec:regimes}

Empirically, \eqref{eq:taylor} governs a \emph{perturbative regime} of mild
compression, and fails---by orders of magnitude, not percentages---beyond a
capability-dependent threshold: the \emph{cliff}. Our measurements place the
cliff for magnitude pruning at $\dens^{*}\!\approx\!0.4$--$0.6$ across model
sizes and capabilities, consistent with prior ability-space observations,
and show that no local expansion at $\wz$ anticipates it
(\S\ref{sec:exp}). Scaling-down laws are therefore \emph{two-regime} laws:
\begin{equation}
\Delta\Lc{c}(\dens) =
\begin{cases}
\varphi\!\big(\gbar{c}^{\top}\dw_{\dens},\,
\dw_{\dens}^{\top}\Fc{c}\dw_{\dens}\big) & \dens > \dens_c^{*}
\quad\text{(perturbative)}\\[2pt]
\text{collapse law, } \Lc{c}\to \Lc{c}^{\varnothing}
& \dens \le \dens_c^{*} \quad\text{(non-perturbative)},
\end{cases}
\label{eq:tworegime}
\end{equation}
and the scientific weight shifts to predicting the boundary
$\dens_c^{*}(N_0, f)$ from measurable statistics of the geometry. Our
leading candidate is a \emph{concentration statistic}: the share
$\kappa_c(q)$ of capability Fisher mass carried by the top-$q$ fraction of
weight coordinates by magnitude. Since magnitude pruning deletes from the
bottom, mass concentrated at the top is deleted last; models whose geometry
is more top-concentrated should be more prunable. Across Qwen3
0.6B/1.7B/4B, $\kappa_c(1\%)$ rises monotonically with $N_0$ while the
cliff moves deeper, and we pre-register the prediction that the anomalously
prune-robust 14B checkpoint exhibits higher $\kappa$ than 32B
(\S\ref{sec:exp}).

\subsection{Claims, laws, and the method-selection map}
\label{sec:claims}

\paragraph{Pre-registered claim hierarchy.}
\textbf{T1 (strong):} with deleted capability mass as the common coordinate,
a single transfer function $\varphi$ covers pruning and quantization (and
distillation through the function basis). \textbf{T2 (fallback):} the
common coordinate holds but $\varphi$ splits by method---still a unified
framework producing a family of laws. Either way the framework differs from
single-method loss-space laws
\citep{busbridge2025distillation,frantar2023scaling,kumar2024scaling} in
five pre-registered respects: capability-vector $y$; a measured link layer
to task success; a recovery axis; forms constrained by independently
measured geometry; and cross-method comparability at matched deleted mass.

\paragraph{Parametric laws.}
The dense reference is a per-capability Chinchilla-style decomposition
$\Lc{c}^{\text{dense}}(N)=E_c + A_c N^{-\alpha_c}$
\citep{hoffmann2022training}; the capability spectrum of exponents
$\alpha_c$ is itself a result. Distillation follows a per-capability
axiomatic template with the teacher entering as $\vec{L}^{\mathcal{T}}$
\citep{busbridge2025distillation}; recovery follows
$\Lc{c}(\Dr)=\Lc{c}^{\infty}+B_c \Dr^{-\beta_c}$
\citep{zhang2024scaling,muralidharan2024compact}, with the geometric
prediction that $\beta_c$ grows with the overlap between recovery-data
gradients and the deleted directions. A \emph{state-function hypothesis}
---that the recovered model depends on the damage geometry and $\Dr$ but not
the compression path---is pre-registered with its discriminating test.

\paragraph{Reduced form and the method-selection map.}
Geometry statistics ($\kappa_c$, $\operatorname{tr}\Fc{c}$, spectrum tail
exponents) are fit as smooth functions of $N_0$ once, then substituted, so
every published law contains only basic parameters
$(N_0, r, \dens, \bits, \Ds, \Dr)$. Intersecting the per-method laws at
matched compression cost yields the \emph{method-selection map}: a partition
of the (compression ratio $\times$ capability requirement) plane into
regions where pruning, quantization, distillation, or a composition is the
cheapest way to retain the required profile---including the empirically
nontrivial region where mild pruning is better than free for
retrieval-style capabilities.
