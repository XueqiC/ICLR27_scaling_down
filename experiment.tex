\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:exp}

We report two kinds of evidence: (a) a forensic re-analysis of a large
prior ability-space compression grid, which motivates the measurement layer;
and (b) preliminary instantiations of capability geometry on the Qwen3
family. The full pre-registered grid (methods $\times$ sizes $\times$
recovery) is in progress; this section will be extended as it lands.

\subsection{Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\textbf{Models.} Qwen3 dense checkpoints at 0.6B, 1.7B, 4B (reported here)
and 8B, 14B, 32B (in progress). \textbf{Probes.} Per capability, prompts
with reference completions from held-out benchmark splits chosen to be
cross-benchmark disjoint from all transfer data: MATH-500 (math), MBPP
(code), 2WikiMultihopQA (multi-hop QA); estimation and measurement halves
are disjoint. \textbf{Compression.} Global magnitude pruning at ten
densities $\dens\in[0.3,0.9]$; round-to-nearest weight quantization
(int8/int4, in progress). \textbf{Geometry.} $\gbar{c}$ and $\Fc{c}$
estimated from 64 per-sample gradients per capability at $\wz$
(\S\ref{sec:geometry}); quadratic forms computed exactly by streaming
$g_i^{\top}\dw$; spectra accumulated in $2\times10^5$ magnitude-quantile
bins.

\subsection{Re-analysis of a prior ability-space grid}
\label{sec:reanalysis}

A prior internal study fit compression laws directly on Rasch abilities
estimated from $\sim$100-item panels over a 774-cell Qwen3 grid
(distillation $\times$ teachers $\times$ sizes $\times$ seeds; pruning;
quantization), and reported the headline negative result that no derived law
had held-out predictive power. Our re-analysis attributes much of this to
the measurement and testing layer rather than to compression itself:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt,topsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{A recipe artifact drove the falsification.} The
pre-registered $r{=}1$ self-distillation anchor (same-size student must not
move) failed only when answer-only distillation---a known, capability-
selective data-composition effect---was pooled with standard recipes.
Stratified, the anchor holds for standard recipes (math: $-3.22$ ability
units for answer-only alone versus $-0.13$ pooled without it), and held-out
transfer-law error drops from $9.0\times$ to $1.8\times$ the noise floor.
\item \textbf{Same-benchmark evaluation inflated gains.} Each domain panel
mixed items from the distillation-trace benchmark with a never-trained
sibling benchmark. Splitting them shows QA distillation gains concentrated
on the trained half (e.g.\ $+0.198$ vs.\ $+0.127$ accuracy for soft
KD)---roughly one third of the headline gain was distribution fit. Math and
code showed no split.
\item \textbf{Interval-scale ability was unsupported.} Item discrimination
is strongly heterogeneous (2PL preferred by $\Delta$AIC $196$--$348$), so
equal-discrimination ability differences are not interval-scaled;
rank-level conclusions survive (1PL/2PL rank correlation $\ge 0.98$).
\item \textbf{Item-level damage is smooth pre-cliff.} Comparing a
uniform-logit-shift model against a discrete item-deletion mixture on
pruning ladders with item-level records, the shift model wins or ties
almost everywhere before the cliff: capability damage does not proceed by
deleting individual testable skills, consistent with distributed
representations and with our smooth pre-cliff loss curves.
\end{enumerate}
These findings fix the design of \S\ref{sec:prelim}: continuous
capability-conditional loss as primary $y$, cross-benchmark probes, and
rank-only use of latent abilities.

\subsection{Capability geometry on Qwen3: preliminary results}
\label{sec:geom-results}

\paragraph{E1: Compression is capability-selective at first order.}
Magnitude pruning improves multi-hop QA loss over a wide range while math
and code degrade: at 4B, $\Delta\Lc{\text{qa}}$ reaches $-1.96$ nats/token
at $\dens=0.55$ and remains negative through $\dens=0.45$, while
$\Delta\Lc{\text{math}}$ rises monotonically. The effect exists at 0.6B
only for mild pruning and strengthens with scale ($-0.25$ at 0.6B,
$-0.79$ at 1.7B, $-1.96$ at 4B). Capability selectivity is thus a
first-order phenomenon that any scalar law must miss.

\paragraph{E2: The signed first-order term predicts the pre-cliff regime.}
The alignment term $\gbar{c}^{\top}\dw$ is negative for pruning across
capabilities (deleting small weights moves downhill on average), and alone
reproduces the QA improvement quantitatively at 4B: predicted $-0.83$
vs.\ measured $-0.79$ at $\dens=0.7$; $-2.43$ vs.\ $-1.76$ at
$\dens=0.6$. Mild-regime math/code effects are likewise tracked at 0.6B--4B.
The full-Fisher quadratic term is currently inflated for long-context
probes by gradient outliers; a trimmed estimator is pre-registered for the
full grid.

\paragraph{E3: The cliff is non-perturbative.}
At $\dens\le 0.5$ (0.6B) and $\dens\le 0.45$ (4B), measured math/code
damage exceeds the full second-order prediction by two orders of magnitude
(e.g.\ 0.6B math at $\dens=0.5$: predicted $-0.19$, measured $+4.68$).
This is not an estimator failure---the same estimator succeeds pre-cliff---
but a regime boundary: collapse is invisible to local geometry, confirming
the two-regime structure of \eqref{eq:tworegime} in loss space itself
(the cliff is not an artifact of discrete metrics or link nonlinearity).

\paragraph{E4: Top-concentration tracks prunability across scale.}
The concentration statistic $\kappa_c(1\%)$---the share of capability
Fisher mass in the top 1\% of coordinates by weight magnitude---rises
monotonically with model size (means $\approx$10\% at 0.6B, 13.6\% at
1.7B, 17.5\% at 4B), while the math cliff onset deepens
($\dens^{*}\approx0.62\to0.60\to0.50$) and the QA-improvement region
widens. Because magnitude pruning deletes bottom coordinates first,
top-concentrated geometry loses less mass at fixed density.
\textbf{Pre-registered prediction:} if concentration governs robustness,
the anomalously prune-robust 14B checkpoint (observed in the prior grid to
be more robust than both 8B and 32B) must show higher $\kappa$ than 32B.
The 8B/14B/32B measurements are queued.

\subsection{Planned full grid}
\label{sec:planned}

The pre-registered grid crosses: sizes 0.6B--32B $\times$
\{magnitude pruning (10 densities), int8/int4 PTQ, distillation onto
smaller Qwen3 students with recipe and $\Ds$ axes\} $\times$ recovery
$\Dr\in\{0, 10\text{M}, 100\text{M}, 1\text{B}\}$ tokens, with all
capability-geometry statistics measured at each source. Analysis follows
the protocol of \S\ref{sec:claims}: pre-registered forms committed with
timestamps before fitting; keep/reject decisions by cell-level paired
bootstrap; cluster bootstrap and measurement-error-weighted fits; repeated
held-out extrapolation on the largest size, deepest compression, and
largest recovery budget; and positive/negative controls (synthetic law
recovery; shuffled capability labels).
