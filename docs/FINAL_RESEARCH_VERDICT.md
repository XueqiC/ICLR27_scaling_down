# Final research verdict for the predictive round

Written 2026-09-15 after the last corner trajectory finished. Every number here is in the ledger,
C73 to C77, with the artifact that produced it.

## The question

Whether a small set of pre-compression observables predicts the conditional loss of unseen
compression and distillation configurations well enough to support a resource decision that can be
checked.

## The verdict, in three parts

**No new predictive relation cleared the bar.** Three permitted structures, one parameter set each
producing the response and both interventions, lost to the strongest of six baselines on every
capability, target and grouped hold-out, including both pre-declared primary targets. The
confirmation package therefore never launched, and the larger student, the second family and the
data-requirement relation, all conditional on it, did not run. One secondary cell replicates across
all three held-out students against a pre-specified baseline and is recorded as the only candidate
worth confirming if the round continued.

**The response is additive in budget and reuse, at fixed student, on the readout with the power to
say otherwise.** The development set contained no complete budget-by-reuse rectangle, so the one
fit-free discriminator between an additive law and an interacting one had never been computable.
Four trajectories designed by update-boundary replay landed every corner to the token on two
students. On the pre-registered primary readout the second difference sits inside the registered
band on both students with the same sign, six to seven times smaller than an interacting structure
predicted. The earlier falsification of the four-coefficient joint form was a falsification of its
logarithmic reuse term, not of additivity, and the profiled curvature near one says the same thing
from the other side.

**The larger student is different where the design had least power.** On math and code the 4B
student shows second differences the 1B does not, at readouts registered as marginal and
underpowered. Under the rule neither is a rejection. The code value, nearly four times the registered
noise, is the round's one unanticipated observation and is filed as a lead.

## What the paper says now

At fixed student, the distillation response is additive in training budget and data reuse to within
pool-seed noise on the primary readout, across two students spanning 4.6 times in non-embedding size.
The reuse term is closer to linear than logarithmic. The cost of over-reusing a small pool transfers
across three QA distributions and grows with student size; the gain from distillation is a 2Wiki
statement. A data requirement phrased as keeping a loss increase below a threshold is supportable in
form; a claim that a fitted law predicts it is not, because no fitted structure beat a low-order
response surface. The pre-compression statistics branch is closed: source information does not help
even when the median configuration curve is held fixed.

## What it would take to go further

A confirmation design that builds at least one budget-by-reuse rectangle by construction; a
candidate whose reuse term is near-linear rather than logarithmic, written down before it is fitted;
and enough replication on code to give it power, since that is where the larger student diverged.
About 39 of the round's 72 GPU-hours remain.
