### C81. A9 pruning measurement efficiency (CPU only, 2026-09-16; results/a9-measurement-efficiency)

QUESTION AND PROVENANCE. Does the five-parameter source-conditioned power form reach fixed prediction-error targets with fewer pruning measurements than per-density OLS plus interpolation (A2) or the source-free median configuration curve? The requested A1/C73 table is actually Gemma distillation data. A9 therefore uses the explicitly enumerated nine-state Pythia pruning panel from V36/V40/V92: 160M/410M/1.4B × 16k/64k/143k × densities 0.6/0.7/0.8/0.9 × math/code/QA, verified against data_mirror. That is 36 measurements per capability (108 scalar labels). Four-density A2 has 16 parameters; the paper's 20 refers to V53's larger five-anchor panel. Source correction, row provenance and input hashes are recorded in the artifacts.

FIXED DESIGN. Before any A9 fit, `plan.json` was written with SHA256 `0b12b06d3a9919c737cdc6b89480dc5461606a981d2ccff2a090a6ee06e07a95`. Nominal fractions 25%/50%/100%, 20 seeds (91000–91019), nested subsets, and separate reduction axes: 2/5/9 sources give 8/20/36 measurements per capability (realized 22.2%/55.6%/100%); 1/2/4 independently sampled densities per source give 9/18/36. Source reduction retains the two seen-test states so the fixed seen-state panel remains genuinely seen. Identical labels/source inputs and zero validation tuning for all predictors; training-only standardization; OLS source coefficients, fixed V53 exponent grid; median and A2 use the same adjacent interpolation/boundary-extension rule. Rank failures are explicit, with no regularization rescue. Targets 0.15/0.25/0.35 nats qualify only at a tested budget with 20/20 fits and median MAE at or below the target.

TESTS. Fixed in-range held-out sets have 2 unseen-density cells per capability on the two seen states and 42 cells on 11 new states, assembled from the pred_full sidecars' V38/V42/V46/V49 artifacts plus V53. Nine new-state and two seen-state boundary cells per capability are scored separately and in all-cell summaries. V72's 18 labelled cells are also scored separately: its two 2.8B stage labels have identical learned weights and unverified training histories, so they are excluded from primary new-state efficiency claims. This is retrospective reuse of historical confirmation labels, not a new blinded confirmation.

RESULT. A limited measurement-efficiency advantage appears under density reduction. Power reaches 0.35 nats on seen-state math with 18 measurements versus A2's 36; on new-state math and code it reaches 0.25 with 18 versus A2's 36 and 0.35 with 9 versus A2's 36. On new-state code at 0.35, power needs 9 versus the median curve's 18. These are six finite comparator/target savings, five against A2 and one against median; the A2 comparisons partly reflect its sparse-budget fit failures. Twelve other comparator/target cases are reached by power but never by the comparator on this grid, so no finite savings estimate is assigned. Source reduction has no finite smaller-budget crossing where both predictors reach the target. No general measurement-efficiency claim follows.

MEDIAN AND LIMITS. Among 18 capability/axis/budget/panel comparisons with all three predictors fitted in all 20 replicates, median is lowest in 4 and power is strictly lowest in 4; median does not dominate everywhere. Median wins new-state QA in every complete three-predictor comparison; power reaches none of the three new-state QA targets. With density reduction median reaches QA 0.35 at 18 measurements. The seen-state interpolation panel has only two cells per capability. IQRs describe subset variation; repeated full-budget fits are identical, not independent evidence. No monotonic smoothing or between-budget interpolation is used.

VALIDATION AND OUTPUTS. 1,080 attempted fits; 222 explicit rank failures. A2 fits 0/20 density-reduction replicates at 9 measurements and 6/20 at 18, per capability; power and median fit all 20 at those budgets. At 8 measurements from two sources both source-conditioned forms fail, while median fits. Checks enforce same labels, source inputs, test-cell hashes, nested single-axis subsets and exact MAE accounting; synthetic tests cover law recovery, rank rejection and interpolation. Full-budget A2 reproduces V42's held-out interpolation predictions within 1.4e-15 nats. Script: `analysis/a9_measurement_efficiency.py`; artifacts: `plan.json`, `summary.json` (all subsets, fits, failures, predictions, scores, IQRs, thresholds and paired differences), `summary.md`, four-page `a9_efficiency.pdf`, and `figs/a9_a.pdf` through `a9_l.pdf` with PNG previews and numeric/style sidecars. A runtime guard confines analysis writes to A9 results and temporary caches. No training, GPU use, paper TeX/figure/table edits by this analysis, or commit.

### C82. A10 distillation data-requirement boundary: bounded, not point identified (CPU, 2026-09-16; results/a10-data-requirement)

TARGET. `D_U,min(T,S,c,j;tau) = inf{D_U: delta_c,j(T,D_U,S) <= tau}`, with signed post-training minus own-initial loss and tolerances 0, 0.1, 0.25 and 0.5 nats. `T` and `D_U` both count supervised completion tokens; `E=T/D_U`. Implementation: [analysis/a10_data_requirement.py](../analysis/a10_data_requirement.py). Reproduce with `python -B analysis/a10_data_requirement.py`; this prints the summary and writes only the A10 result directory. No training or model loading.

INVENTORY. Read 1,178 eval/snapshot files across results and the data mirror, covering 156 run identities; deduplicated 546 eval aliases and 168 scope aliases. The 2,076 readout rows retain missing-accounting and discarded-launch observations explicitly. There are 1,338 usable positive-budget rows with verified distinct-pool supervised accounting, including 654 controlled matrix, critical-region and corner readouts. The historical V39 Pythia runs are included in the census; V94 and V97/V100 training artifacts live under v12-distill. Different protocols and legacy/fresh 2Wiki probes remain separate. Older logs without a distinct-pool audit do not receive an invented D_U. The five discarded matrix launches do not enter inference. All counts and original paths are in [summary.json](../results/a10-data-requirement/summary.json).

IDENTIFIABILITY. There are zero exact-budget cells comparing distinct pool-size rungs, hence zero exact-budget crossing brackets. Recorded MATH-500, MBPP, legacy/fresh 2Wiki, MuSiQue and TriviaQA readouts are **bounded only**. Math/code on the three QA distributions are **not identifiable**, because those capability/distribution scores were never measured. A separate, explicitly approximate analysis selects one observed checkpoint per trajectory/readout/budget band and requires max(T)/min(T) <= 1.01. It finds 52 local monotone threshold crossings, 80 non-monotone threshold cells and 15 threshold cells with replicate disagreement; the 5% matching sensitivity finds 34 crossings, 156 non-monotone cells and 18 disagreements. These are cell-by-tolerance counts, not independent replicates. Crossings remain intervals; uncrossed cells remain one-sided constraints; non-monotone cells retain feasible components and transition brackets. A failure-only lower bound requires monotone continuation; passing points give upper bounds without assuming monotonicity. No precise empirical minimum is fitted to uncrossed data.

PREDICTION. Fit a low-degree censored log-linear boundary in log T and categorical student terms, with a fixed weak ridge selecting a representative of potentially nonunique solutions. Compare with training-fold-selected fixed reuse `D_U=T/E*` and per-student decreasing isotonic interpolation at the nearest training budget. Every fold rebuilds the training constraints and fits; finite parametric predictions abstain when that student has no training crossing. Leave-one-budget-out removes a whole budget band; leave-one-pool-out removes the pool hash across students and checkpoints; an additional leave-one-pool-seed-out removes that seed across sizes. Exact-pool holdouts cannot contain a crossing bracket, so their one-sided violation is never labelled boundary MAE. Boundary error is reported as optimistic/pessimistic distance in log D_U to held-out crossing brackets; delta errors use the same folds, with range-normalized comparisons on the same scored crossing rows and a common-target comparison across all three methods. **Greater stability of boundary prediction is not established for any recorded capability/distribution.** Small optimistic errors inside broad brackets are not accurate point predictions.

PROPOSED CONFIRMATION, NOT LAUNCHED. Eighteen trajectories: two students x three pool positions x three fresh pool seeds, evaluating each trajectory at 50k/100k/200k actual supervised tokens and its own initial reference. At planning tolerance 0.25 and budget 200k, joint candidate/baseline contributions select supervised pool targets of 9k/32k/343k for 1B and 9k/38k/315k for 4B (below/near/above the joint design envelope). All four tolerances and all readouts should be registered and evaluated. The upper positions extend observed support; they do not guarantee a zero-tolerance crossing, particularly on TriviaQA. Count supervised labels over the entire tokenized pool before training and log distinct supervised tokens actually seen separately: the upper pools exceed the final budget, so their D_U cannot be reconstructed from a completed first epoch. Selection rules, model abstentions, source paths and all 18 proposed trajectories are recorded in summary.json.
