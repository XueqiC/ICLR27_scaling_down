### C81. A9 pruning measurement efficiency (CPU only, 2026-09-16; results/a9-measurement-efficiency)

QUESTION AND PROVENANCE. Does the five-parameter source-conditioned power form reach fixed prediction-error targets with fewer pruning measurements than per-density OLS plus interpolation (A2) or the source-free median configuration curve? The requested A1/C73 table is actually Gemma distillation data. A9 therefore uses the explicitly enumerated nine-state Pythia pruning panel from V36/V40/V92: 160M/410M/1.4B × 16k/64k/143k × densities 0.6/0.7/0.8/0.9 × math/code/QA, verified against data_mirror. That is 36 measurements per capability (108 scalar labels). Four-density A2 has 16 parameters; the paper's 20 refers to V53's larger five-anchor panel. Source correction, row provenance and input hashes are recorded in the artifacts.

FIXED DESIGN. Before any A9 fit, `plan.json` was written with SHA256 `0b12b06d3a9919c737cdc6b89480dc5461606a981d2ccff2a090a6ee06e07a95`. Nominal fractions 25%/50%/100%, 20 seeds (91000–91019), nested subsets, and separate reduction axes: 2/5/9 sources give 8/20/36 measurements per capability (realized 22.2%/55.6%/100%); 1/2/4 independently sampled densities per source give 9/18/36. Source reduction retains the two seen-test states so the fixed seen-state panel remains genuinely seen. Identical labels/source inputs and zero validation tuning for all predictors; training-only standardization; OLS source coefficients, fixed V53 exponent grid; median and A2 use the same adjacent interpolation/boundary-extension rule. Rank failures are explicit, with no regularization rescue. Targets 0.15/0.25/0.35 nats qualify only at a tested budget with 20/20 fits and median MAE at or below the target.

TESTS. Fixed in-range held-out sets have 2 unseen-density cells per capability on the two seen states and 42 cells on 11 new states, assembled from the pred_full sidecars' V38/V42/V46/V49 artifacts plus V53. Nine new-state and two seen-state boundary cells per capability are scored separately and in all-cell summaries. V72's 18 labelled cells are also scored separately: its two 2.8B stage labels have identical learned weights and unverified training histories, so they are excluded from primary new-state efficiency claims. This is retrospective reuse of historical confirmation labels, not a new blinded confirmation.

RESULT. A limited measurement-efficiency advantage appears under density reduction. Power reaches 0.35 nats on seen-state math with 18 measurements versus A2's 36; on new-state math and code it reaches 0.25 with 18 versus A2's 36 and 0.35 with 9 versus A2's 36. On new-state code at 0.35, power needs 9 versus the median curve's 18. These are six finite comparator/target savings, five against A2 and one against median; the A2 comparisons partly reflect its sparse-budget fit failures. Twelve other comparator/target cases are reached by power but never by the comparator on this grid, so no finite savings estimate is assigned. Source reduction has no finite smaller-budget crossing where both predictors reach the target. No general measurement-efficiency claim follows.

MEDIAN AND LIMITS. Among 18 capability/axis/budget/panel comparisons with all three predictors fitted in all 20 replicates, median is lowest in 4 and power is strictly lowest in 4; median does not dominate everywhere. Median wins new-state QA in every complete three-predictor comparison; power reaches none of the three new-state QA targets. With density reduction median reaches QA 0.35 at 18 measurements. The seen-state interpolation panel has only two cells per capability. IQRs describe subset variation; repeated full-budget fits are identical, not independent evidence. No monotonic smoothing or between-budget interpolation is used.

VALIDATION AND OUTPUTS. 1,080 attempted fits; 222 explicit rank failures. A2 fits 0/20 density-reduction replicates at 9 measurements and 6/20 at 18, per capability; power and median fit all 20 at those budgets. At 8 measurements from two sources both source-conditioned forms fail, while median fits. Checks enforce same labels, source inputs, test-cell hashes, nested single-axis subsets and exact MAE accounting; synthetic tests cover law recovery, rank rejection and interpolation. Full-budget A2 reproduces V42's held-out interpolation predictions within 1.4e-15 nats.
