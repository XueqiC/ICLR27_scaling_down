\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
\label{sec:discussion}

What can be predicted now is specific. Given the parameter count, the
pretraining token count, and the dense capability losses of a source
in the development range, the pruning form of Eq.~\eqref{eq:power}
predicts the math and code response at any density inside the fitted
range to within a few tenths of a nat, and a per-density regression
does the same without the power structure. Given the same inputs, the
frozen surface of Eq.~\eqref{eq:quant2d} predicts the grouped
quantizer at bit-widths and group sizes it was not fit on, for the
states it was fit on, with its curvature fixed by regularization.
Given a student's state, a linear form predicts its math transfer,
and under a fixed distillation protocol a response model in budget,
reuse, and a student descriptor predicts unseen data pools on the two
development students. Each comes with coefficients
(Appendix~\ref{sec:app_coef}) and a tested range, and
\S\ref{sec:selection} connects them to an explicit selection problem.

What cannot be predicted is equally specific. No source-conditioned
form predicts the QA response to pruning on a new checkpoint, and no
form transfers to density 0.55 or to a source five times larger than
the development range. Bit-width alone supports no continuous
quantization relation, and the separable candidate we tested is worse
than the surface. Joint transfer of the distillation forms to a larger
student and a new pool is unreliable, and distillation lowers the
conditional loss on the primary QA distribution without that change
carrying over to TriviaQA, so the QA result is a prediction relation
on the measured distribution and no general QA gain
(Appendix~\ref{sec:app_measure}).

The dense capability anchor is the input to keep, the working forms
are low-dimensional with exponents not established as necessary, and
transfer across sizes is bounded by the development range, so a wider
development panel is the direct route to wider laws. The limitations
are small controlled panels with correlated observations, one
quantizer family and one teacher, a loss endpoint whose code
specificity was not detected on a secondary benchmark, and a selection
validation with nominal storage as the only cost.
