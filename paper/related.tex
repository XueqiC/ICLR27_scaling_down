\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Scaling laws for compression.} Existing compression laws
take sparsity \citep{frantar2023scaling}, precision
\citep{kumar2024scaling}, training formats \citep{panferov2025unified},
the pre-pruning loss and post-training tokens \citep{zhang2024scaling},
or the teacher loss \citep{busbridge2025distillation} as inputs and
predict an aggregate loss; we follow the P$^2$ law in admitting the
pre-compression loss and add a per-capability endpoint measured the
same way for three methods, a development panel in which size and
stage vary separately, and held-out tests with same-input baselines.

\paragraph{Compression methods and their task-conditioned evaluation.}
The fixed operations stand in for method families: pruning
\citep{lecun1989optimal,hassibi1992second,han2015deep,frankle2019lottery,frantar2023sparsegpt,sun2023simple,ma2023llmpruner,ashkboos2024slicegpt,men2024shortgpt},
post-training quantization
\citep{dettmers2022llmint8,frantar2022gptq,xiao2022smoothquant,lin2023awq,chee2023quip,dettmers2023spqr,shao2023omniquant,dettmers2023qlora},
and distillation
\citep{hinton2015distilling,sanh2019distilbert,hsieh2023distilling,mukherjee2023orca,gu2023minillm,agarwal2023gkd,xu2024survey}.
Pruning disproportionately harms knowledge-intensive tasks
\citep{jaiswal2023compressing}, low-bit quantization harms reasoning
\citep{liu2025quantization} and favors undertrained models
\citep{ouyang2024lowbit}, compression shifts trust dimensions unevenly
\citep{hong2024decoding}, and selective truncation can help
\citep{sharma2023laser}. Laws with
task-level targets exist: \citet{sengupta2025compression} fit pruning
laws to test loss and averaged zero-shot accuracy against the pruning
ratio and recovery tokens, with per-task exponents and transfer to
unseen models in their revised version; \citet{zhou2025task} stratify
post-training quantization laws into memorization, application, and
reasoning strata for one quantizer; task-specific distillation has
been mapped over dataset size, compression ratio, and supervision
format \citep{ghita2026task}. Our relations use a
continuous conditional loss, admit the dense capability anchor and
training tokens as inputs, and treat the three methods with one
protocol.

\paragraph{Fitting and validating empirical laws.} Pretraining loss follows power laws in parameters and data
\citep{hestness2017deep,kaplan2020scaling,hoffmann2022training};
downstream accuracy is predictable mainly through continuous
log-likelihood measurements
\citep{wei2022emergent,schaeffer2023emergent,schaeffer2024predicting,gadre2024language,isik2024scaling,bhagia2024establishing,lourie2025scaling},
which motivates our loss endpoint and its stated boundaries; fitted
scaling-law forms are fragile to the fitting protocol
\citep{li2025misfitting,besiroglu2024chinchilla}, and repeated tokens
are worth less than new tokens in data-constrained pretraining
\citep{muennighoff2023scaling}, an effect our reuse-count coordinate
measures in the distillation setting. Gradient-based importance \citep{lecun1989optimal,frantar2023sparsegpt,sun2023simple}
enters here only as mechanism evidence (Appendix~\ref{sec:app_mech}).
