Selection rules:
Distillation cohort exactly follows v41: gpt-5.6-luna_full_75_uxseen*, gpt-5.6-luna_full_600_uxseen*, gpt-5.6-luna_full_225_lora_dseed*; 18 endpoint and 12 U225 nonzero trajectory snapshots. Final evals supply/verify dense anchors; final training endpoints and other recipes are not added to the frozen-fit cohort. E = matching train_log.loss_curve.tokens_seen / unique_data_pool_tokens; delta = snapshot.post_training[cap] - snapshot.dense[cap].
Constant = fits.constant[0]; E-only = fits.E[0] + fits.E[1] * natural_log(E). Coefficients are read unchanged, never refitted. Recovered E ranges and U225 MAEs are checked against v41. U75 uxseenE low-budget runs are included in its 18-point fit cohort.
Pruning and quantization: protocol A, Pythia 1B and 6.9B at step112000; rows selected by cap without pooling capabilities. Density points 0.9, 0.8, 0.75, 0.7, 0.65, 0.6, 0.55; shade d < 0.6. Plot recorded actual and pred fields. 6.9B uses squares and lighter opacity; its power curve is omitted per the requested overlay.
Integer bits are equally spaced categories [8, 6, 5, 4, 3]. Measured points are filled; full predictions are hollow, with thin dashed guides only; per-bit median uses dotted guides. Every 5-bit row must have rule='interp(4,6)'. No continuous bit-width law is drawn.

QA U225 budget 2: mean completion tokens=280059.0, n=6 snapshots; E-only bias (prediction minus observed)=-0.477500431 nats/token.

Limitations:
There is no measured density 0.85 in the selected v49 files; no point is interpolated there. The displayed median_curve is the specifically requested baseline, not claimed best for every source/regime.
Trajectory checkpoints within a run share training and probes; no independent-seed error bars are inferred. T-only and 2D curves are not requested and are not reduced to an E curve by inventing a token budget.
