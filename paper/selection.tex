\section{From Laws to Capability-Specific Selection}
\label{sec:selection}


Given an initial model, a capability, and a resource constraint,
which method and setting preserves that capability best? We write the
decision as
\begin{equation}
(m^{*},\boldsymbol\theta_m^{*})=\arg\min_{m,\;\boldsymbol\theta_m\in\mathcal F_m(B)}\;
\widehat L_{m,c}(\mathbf x,\boldsymbol\theta_m),
\label{eq:selection}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal F_m(B)$ is the set of settings of method $m$ that meet
the budget $B$. Every candidate is compared through the absolute loss of the deployed
model (the source with its predicted response, or the smaller student
after distillation); the map for one capability need not coincide
with the map for another.

We validated this use retrospectively on the controlled panel with a
nominal storage budget $r_{\max}$ from 0.2 to 1, using only measured configurations (211 compression cells and 9
same-stage smaller students across 17 states) and refitting every law
with the target state held out. A policy with no
feasible configuration at a budget returns no choice: quantization is
feasible everywhere, whereas pruning-only covers 56\% and
distillation-only 33\% of the 289 state-budget cells. Over all cells
the choice made from the laws is within 0.19 (math), 0.17 (code), and
0.42 (QA) nats of the measured oracle, and within 0.03 nats for the
worst of the three capabilities, picking the oracle's method in
96\%, 95\%, 73\%, and 94\% of cells; the cheapest feasible
configuration loses 4.3 to 5.2 nats. A quantization-only policy is
close behind (0.21, 0.19, 0.49, and 0.04 nats), because on this
storage axis quantization has the most feasible cells and dominates
math and code; the maps' contribution is therefore the connection of
the laws to an explicit decision, and they show no general advantage
over a strong single method on this panel. Where a smaller student exists, distillation-only is near the oracle
for QA (0.06 nats; Appendix Fig.~\ref{fig:selection}). Under a heuristic rule (predicted gap between the two best methods
smaller than the larger of their held-out errors) about half of the
cells have no clear winner; the oracle's method lies in the reported
candidate set in every such cell, and the rule carries no coverage
guarantee (Appendix~\ref{sec:app_selection}). The maps inherit the laws' boundaries: in-range states, nominal
storage as the only cost, and unreliable QA cells.
