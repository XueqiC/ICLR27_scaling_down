\section{From Laws to Capability-Specific Selection}
\label{sec:selection}


Given an initial model, a capability, and a resource constraint,
which method and setting preserves that capability best? We write the
decision as
\begin{equation}
(m^{*},\boldsymbol\theta_m^{*})=\arg\min_{m,\;\boldsymbol\theta_m\in\mathcal F_m(B)}\;
\widehat L_{m,c}(\mathbf x,\boldsymbol\theta_m),
\label{eq:selection}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal F_m(B)$ is the set of settings of method $m$ that meet
the budget $B$. Every candidate is compared through the absolute loss of the deployed
model; the map for one capability need not coincide with the map for
another.

We validated this use retrospectively on the controlled panel with a
nominal storage budget $r_{\max}$ from 0.2 to 1, over measured
configurations of 17 states with every law refit with the target
state held out. A policy with no feasible configuration returns no choice (pruning-only
covers 56\% and distillation-only 33\% of the 289 cells). The choice
made from the laws is within 0.19, 0.17, and 0.42 nats of the measured
oracle for math, code, and QA, and within 0.03 for the worst of the
three, picking the oracle's method in 96\%, 95\%, 73\%, and 94\% of
cells (cheapest-feasible loses 4.3 to 5.2 nats). A quantization-only policy is close behind (0.21, 0.19, 0.49, and
0.04 nats), because on this storage axis quantization has the most
feasible cells and dominates math and code. Where a smaller student exists, distillation-only is near the oracle
for QA; under a heuristic no-clear-winner rule about half of the
cells report a candidate set that always contains the oracle's method
(Appendix~\ref{sec:app_selection}).

\paragraph{Independent confirmation of the locked rule.} We then locked the complete rule of Table~\ref{tab:final}, froze
every candidate's predicted loss and the four maps for four fresh
Pythia states (160M, 410M, and 1.4B at step 32k; 1B at step 64k; 18
configurations each, weights hashed against every earlier revision),
and only then measured. Over 68 state-budget cells per
capability, the locked-rule maps are within 0.00001, 0.004, and 0.14
nats of the oracle for math, code, and QA, below the earlier
source-conditioned maps (0.015, 0.11, 0.21) and quantization-only
(0.007, 0.010, 0.25), which meets the pre-stated criterion; the
max-over-capabilities map trails the earlier map by 0.0002 nats and
is reported as retrospective. The QA map differs from the math and
code maps, choosing pruning at high budgets on the 32k states and
distillation wherever a student exists, with the oracle following it
(Appendix~\ref{sec:app_selection}, Fig.~\ref{fig:rule_maps}).
