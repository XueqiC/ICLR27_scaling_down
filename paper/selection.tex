\section{From Laws to Capability-Specific Selection}
\label{sec:selection}


Given a reference model, a capability, and a budget, which method
and setting preserves that capability best? The decision is
\begin{equation}
(m^{*},\boldsymbol\theta_m^{*})=\arg\min_{m,\;\boldsymbol\theta_m\in\mathcal F_m(B)}\;
\widehat L_{m,c}(\mathbf x,\boldsymbol\theta_m),
\label{eq:selection}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal F_m(B)$ holds the settings of method $m$ within
budget $B$. 



\paragraph{Retrospective validation.} On the controlled panel (17
states, budgets from 0.2 to 1, every law refit with the target held
out) the law-based choice is within 0.19, 0.17, and 0.42 nats of the
oracle for math, code, and QA, picking the oracle's method in 96\%,
95\%, and 73\% of 289 cells; quantization-only is close behind (0.21,
0.19, 0.49).

\paragraph{Prospective confirmation of a frozen selection rule.} We then locked a complete selection rule (Appendix
Table~\ref{tab:locked_rule}; it uses source-free predictors on new
states, including the per-bit median rather than the per-bit
regression of Table~\ref{tab:final}), froze every candidate's
predicted loss and the four maps for four fresh Pythia states (160M,
410M, and 1.4B at step 32k; 1B at step 64k; 18 configurations each,
weights hashed against every earlier revision), and only then
measured (Fig.~\ref{fig:rule_main}). Over 17 budgets on each state (68 cells per objective from four
models), the frozen-rule maps are within 0.00001, 0.004, and 0.14
nats of the oracle for math, code, and QA (2Wiki), below the
source-conditioned maps (0.015, 0.11, 0.21) and quantization-only
under the same candidates and rule (0.007, 0.010, 0.25), meeting the registered criterion (mean regret over the 68 cells within the source-conditioned maps' and below quantization-only's); for the largest loss increase the first condition fails (0.0020 against 0.0019; quantization-only 0.008), so that evaluation is prospective and not confirmed. The QA confirmation is an aggregate: the frozen
rule beats quantization-only on three states and trails it on
160M@32k (0.22 against 0.13). The math and code margins over
quantization-only come almost entirely from 1B@64k, the state with
historical distillation candidates; on the three newly measured 32k states the mean regrets aggregated over their 51 cells differ by less than $10^{-5}$ nats (Table~\ref{tab:rule_decomp}).  The pruning and quantization candidates are new measurements; the distillation candidates are two existing same-stage students purged from the fit that predicts them.
