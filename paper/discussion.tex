\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
\label{sec:discussion}

Table~\ref{tab:final} (Appendix~\ref{sec:app_results}) answers the same five questions for each arm:
what predictor is delivered, which pre-compression inputs it needs,
which parts were frozen and which choices are retrospective, where it
gains over the strongest same-information baseline, and where it does
not apply. The pattern across arms is consistent. Source-conditioned
forms add value at unseen configurations of seen states (pruning
densities, quantization group sizes, distillation code at a new pool
size), and on new model states the source-free references are as good
or better: the median density curve on 2.8B and 6.9B, the
per-configuration median on the new quantization state, the constant
on the held-out student. QA is carried by source-free predictors on
every arm, and its distillation gain is repeatable within the 2Wiki
distribution and reverses sign on MuSiQue and TriviaQA
(Appendix~\ref{sec:app_measure}). \S\ref{sec:selection} connects the
laws to an explicit selection problem.

The dense capability anchor is the input to keep, the working forms
are low-dimensional, and transfer across sizes is unreliable. The
limitations are small correlated panels, one quantizer family and one
teacher, and nominal storage as the only selection cost.
