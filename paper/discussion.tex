\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
\label{sec:discussion}

Table~\ref{tab:final} (Appendix~\ref{sec:app_results}) summarizes each arm's delivery, inputs, frozen parts, gains, and limits. Across arms, source-conditioned forms add value at unseen configurations of seen states, source-free references are as good or better on new states, and QA is carried by source-free predictors.

Our relations are conditional on the evaluated loss distributions and
compression implementations; gains on 2Wiki do not establish general QA gains. Transfer to unseen source
states remains uneven, and simple configuration-level response
estimates can outperform source-conditioned parametric forms. The decision study covers four Pythia states under nominal storage
budgets, with new pruning and quantization measurements and
previously evaluated distillation candidates excluded from predictor
fitting.
