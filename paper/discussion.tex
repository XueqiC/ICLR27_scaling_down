\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
\label{sec:discussion}

Table~\ref{tab:final} (Appendix~\ref{sec:app_results}) answers, per
arm, what is delivered, which inputs it needs, which parts were
frozen, where it gains over the strongest same-information baseline,
and where it does not apply.
The pattern is consistent: source-conditioned forms add value at
unseen configurations of seen states, source-free references are as
good or better on new model states, and QA is carried by source-free predictors on every arm.

Our relations are conditional on the evaluated loss distributions and
compression implementations; improvements on 2Wiki do not establish
general QA improvements. Transfer to unseen source
states remains uneven, and simple configuration-level response
estimates can outperform source-conditioned parametric forms. The decision study covers four Pythia states under nominal storage
budgets, with new pruning and quantization measurements and
previously evaluated distillation candidates excluded from predictor
fitting.
