\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
\label{sec:discussion}

Table~\ref{tab:final} (Appendix~\ref{sec:app_results}) answers, per
arm, what predictor is delivered, which inputs it needs, which parts
were frozen and which choices are retrospective, where it gains over
the strongest same-information baseline, and where it does not apply.
The pattern is consistent: source-conditioned forms add value at
unseen configurations of seen states, and on new model states the
source-free references are as good or better. QA is carried by
source-free predictors on every arm, and its distillation gain is
repeatable within the 2Wiki distribution and reverses sign on MuSiQue
and TriviaQA (Appendix~\ref{sec:app_measure}); \S\ref{sec:selection}
connects the relations to an explicit selection problem.

The reference anchor is the input to keep, the working forms are
low-dimensional, and transfer across sizes is unreliable; the
limitations are small correlated panels, one quantizer family and one
teacher, and nominal storage as the only selection cost.
